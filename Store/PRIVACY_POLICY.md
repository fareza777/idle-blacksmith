# Privacy Policy — Emberforge: Idle Blacksmith

**Effective date:** September 2025

Emberforge: Idle Blacksmith ("the game") is a single-player idle game by CozyForge.

## Data the game collects

**None.** The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- No account, login, email, name, or location is requested or stored.
- All progress is saved **locally on your device only**. It is never uploaded.
- No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
- The game makes no network requests during normal play.

## Android features used

- **Share button**: opens the standard Android share sheet so you can send the
  store link to a friend. Nothing is shared unless you choose it yourself.
- **Rate button**: opens the game's Play Store page. No data is sent by the game.

## Children's privacy

The game collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at the same URL as this
