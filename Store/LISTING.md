## Full description (4000 chars max)

Welcome to **Emberforge** — a cozy idle blacksmith game where a single forge grows into a living, breathing smithy village.

**THE FORGE NEVER SLEEPS**
Your smith hammers swords from dawn to dusk — and keeps working while you're away. Come back to a rack full of blades and a chest full of gold. Build a Storehouse to bank even more offline earnings.

**A VILLAGE THAT GROWS WITH YOU**
- **Smithy** — the heart of it all. Upgrade from a roadside anvil to a grand forge.
- **Ore Mine** — hire miners who haul ore straight to your stockpile.
- **Trading Post** — more customers, better prices.
- **Dungeon Gate** — send delvers on timed expeditions for gold and relic ore.
- **Sanctum** — carve runes that permanently boost prices, speed and luck.
- **Blast Furnace** — forced air means faster forging.
- **Storehouse** — lock in bigger offline profits while you sleep.

**FORGE NINE LEGENDARY BLADES**
From humble Copper Daggers to the Voidreaver itself — each recipe unlocks deeper crafting, and every blade can roll Common to Legendary. Watch the hammer fall, quench the steel, and hear that sweet ring of coin.

**A WORLD THAT FEELS ALIVE**
Miners chip rock, porters haul crates, customers browse and carry swords home, a cat naps by the hearth, fireflies rise at dusk, birds cross the sky, and a lucky ember drifts by — tap it for gold. Day turns to night; lamps flicker on; the forge glows.

**A STORY IN THE MARGINS**
Bram Ironroot, Nyx the gatekeeper, Petra Flint and more — a cast of characters with real voices and real opinions, appearing across 20+ story beats as your forge grows.

**DEEP IDLE PROGRESSION**
- Quests and lifetime achievements with permanent bonuses
- Merchant orders and VIP patrons during Rush Hour
- Timed dungeon expeditions
- Runes, talents and relic ore upgrades
- Daily ember rewards
- Prestige: rekindle the forge, keep the power

**NO ADS. NO ENERGY. NO PAY-TO-WIN.**
Just you, the fire, and the next sword.

Download Emberforge and strike while it's hot!
